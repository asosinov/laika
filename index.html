01The platform

Three components, fully integrated and tested at the factory

A modular, highly configurable power skid that arrives as one commissioned product instead of a nine-vendor integration project.

Laika Gateway

AC + DC switchboard connecting your low-voltage utility service and on-site generation.

Laika Inverter

800kW – 3.2MW at 800–950VDC bidirectional, 98.5% peak efficiency, with N+2 redundant power modules and distributed redundancy at the cabinet level.

Laika Link

Optional. Adds galvanic isolation, expands the DC voltage range to 150 – 1000VDC, and enables DC coupling of energy storage.
